Local vs cloud clipping, honestly

Cloud clippers are good, and this page is not going to pretend otherwise. Here is what you actually gain and give up by running the models yourself.

Side by side

  Clips Studio (local) Typical cloud clipper
Cost Free. You pay for electricity. Monthly, usually metered by minutes or clips.
Your footage Never leaves your computer. Uploaded and processed on their servers.
Volume Unlimited. Re-run a stream as often as you like. Capped by your plan.
Speed Bound by your GPU. A long stream is a background job. Usually faster — it is their hardware, at scale.
Editorial quality Good, and scales with the model your GPU can run. Often better in absolute terms — frontier models.
Setup An installer plus a one-time model download. Sign up and paste a link.
Control MIT licensed. Prompts are editable text files. What the product offers.

Where cloud genuinely wins

  • Raw editorial taste. A frontier model has better judgement about what makes a hook land than a model sized to fit a consumer graphics card. That gap is real.
  • Speed on demand. No queue behind your own GPU, and no laptop fans at full tilt for an hour.
  • Nothing to install. If you clip occasionally and would rather not think about VRAM, a subscription is a perfectly reasonable answer.

How a local tool competes anyway

Not by pretending a small model out-reasons a large one. By changing what the model has to be right about.

  • Find more candidates. Audio and visual peaks become clips in their own right, so laughter and chaos are not missed just because the transcript reads flat.
  • Rank, don't grade. Small models score everything in a narrow band, so the finalists are compared against each other in one pass. Relative judgement is far easier than absolute scoring.
  • Keep the human. You review clips before posting either way. The model only has to get good moments into the shortlist — you pick the winners, and that is the cheapest quality multiplier there is.
  • Scale with hardware. A bigger GPU means a bigger model, one click, no new subscription.

Who this is actually for

High volume

Clipping several long streams a week, where metered plans get expensive fast.

Sensitive footage

Unreleased videos, sponsor reads, anything you would rather not hand to a third party.

People who like owning things

If the idea of tuning the scoring prompt yourself sounds good rather than exhausting, you are in the right place.
